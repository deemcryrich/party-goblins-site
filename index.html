Party Goblins

Dare your friends. Last one to finish pays.

How it works

Start a group and share its invite code. Friends join free on their own phones. When someone starts a dare, everyone races to finish it, with a photo for proof if you like. The last player left takes the punishment. Sliders set how hard the dares are and how bad the punishments get.

Questions

How much does it cost?
Joining a group is free. Starting a group is a one-time in-app purchase per group. There's no subscription.

I paid but my group wasn't created.
Close and reopen the app, then tap Create again. Your purchase is saved as a group credit, so you won't be charged twice. If it still doesn't work, email us with the date of the purchase.

A punishment goes too far.
Anyone in the group can tap Veto to replace it with a different one at the same level.

How do I report or block someone?
Long-press their chat message, or open their proof photo, and choose Report or Block. Blocked players' messages and photos are hidden from you. We review every report within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, tap your name at the top of the home screen, then Delete account. This removes your profile, your chat messages and proof photos, and your place in every group.

Play safe

Dares that involve strangers always mean asking first, and a "no" means find someone else. Keep it legal, never drink and drive, and know your group's limits.

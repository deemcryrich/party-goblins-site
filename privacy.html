Privacy Policy

Party Goblins · Effective October 5, 2026

Party Goblins is a multiplayer dare game made by Eurus Production LLC. To let friends play together on their own phones, some information is stored on our servers. This policy explains what, why, and how to remove it.

What we collect

  • Email address, to send your sign-in code. There are no passwords.
  • Display name you choose, shown to the people in your groups.
  • Game activity: groups you create or join, dares, who finished and when, punishments, and win/loss counts.
  • Content you post: chat messages, proof photos you take, and dares or punishments you add to a group.
  • Players you block, so their content stays hidden from you.
  • Purchase records: the App Store transaction ID of each group you buy, so it can't be used twice. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details.
  • Reports you file about other players' content.

Who can see it

Your display name, game activity, chat messages, and proof photos are visible only to members of the same group. Groups are private and joined by invite code. Proof photos are stored privately and shown only to group members.

What we don't do

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, and we never sell your data.

Services we use

  • Supabase hosts the database, sign-in, and photo storage.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers the sign-in code emails.
  • Apple processes in-app purchases.

Each handles data only to provide its service to us.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to take a proof photo. If you can't use the camera, you can pick a photo from your library instead. Only the photo you choose is uploaded.

Deleting your data

In the app, tap your name on the home screen, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, sign-in, chat messages, proof photos, unused group credits, blocks, and group memberships. Game history in your groups keeps automatic event lines (like "Sam finished 1st"), which may still show the display name you used. Reports you filed are kept for safety review. You can also email us to request deletion.

Children

Party Goblins is for people 18 and older and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
